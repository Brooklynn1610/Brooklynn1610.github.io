My story

Making technology useful in everyday life

I'm a Computer Science student at Seattle Pacific University, after earning my Associate of Science in Computer Science at Seattle Central College.

My interest in CS started with hardware. During an AI-IoT hackathon I led a team that built a smart farming system: Arduino boards, sensors and an MQTT network that let people monitor plants and control pumps, lights and fans from a website or phone. Since then I've kept building at the intersection of devices and software, including a Raspberry Pi smart home system with a Flask back end.

I also enjoy the design side: crafting landing pages, planning app experiences like AcademiaOS, and making small playful web apps like a cat trivia game. I like working in teams, making clear plans and helping everyone get to the finish line.
