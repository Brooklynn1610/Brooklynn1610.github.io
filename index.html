About Me

Hi, I am Tu Binh Truong. I am a Computer Science student at Seattle Pacific University, where I am working toward my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with an expected graduation in June 2026. Before transferring to SPU, I earned my Associate of Science in Computer Science at Seattle Central College.

My interest in computer science started with hardware. During an AI-IoT hackathon, I led a team that built a smart farming system using Arduino boards, sensors and MQTT. That project showed me how software can solve real, everyday problems, and I have kept building at the intersection of devices and software ever since.

I enjoy working in teams, making clear plans and helping everyone reach the finish line. I program in C++, Python, Java, SQL and JavaScript, and I am currently looking for software engineering opportunities.
